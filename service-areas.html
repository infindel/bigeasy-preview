Where Does Big Easy Land Clearing Work?

Big Easy Land Clearing is based in New Orleans and works across four core areas: New Orleans itself, Metairie, Baton Rouge, and the Northshore. Southeast Louisiana is not one kind of property. A lot in the city fights different constraints than ten acres outside Covington, and a suburban yard in Metairie calls for a different approach than an open parcel near Baton Rouge. Lot size, tree cover, drainage, and access all change from one area to the next, and our crew adjusts the equipment and the plan to match. We also take on jobs in the smaller communities around each of these four areas. If your property sits somewhere between them, call and ask. Every job starts the same way regardless of address: a walk of the property, a plain explanation of what it needs, and one estimate that's free, fast, and accurate.

Four areas, one crew

Land Clearing By Area

New Orleans

City infill lots are rarely generous. Fences sit close to the line, mature trees crowd old foundations, and equipment access is often a single narrow gate. We work these tight city lots every week, from a single tree removal to a full clearing ahead of new construction.

Land clearing in New Orleans →

Metairie

Metairie lots run tight and suburban, with houses set close together and drainage that has to keep working through the job. We keep equipment compact, protect what's staying, and clear what's coming down without tearing up the rest of the yard.

Land clearing in Metairie →

Baton Rouge

Properties around Baton Rouge tend to run bigger, whether it's a rural homesite, acreage headed for development, or land that's simply grown up over the years. Larger parcels mean more ground to cover, and that's where our equipment and forestry mulching earn their keep.

Land clearing in Baton Rouge →

The Northshore

North of the lake, the acreage is often wooded, and the job is as much about clearing timber as it is about opening usable ground. Forestry mulching handles the brush and smaller trees in one pass, and we bring in the right equipment for anything larger.

Land clearing on the Northshore →
