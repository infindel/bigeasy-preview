The approach

How Do We Clear Dense Brush?

Brush clearing is close, ground-level work. Vines, saplings, thicket, and undergrowth get cut and cleared with crews and equipment sized to the job, not a single machine run over the whole parcel. Fence lines and property corners get walked and marked before anything comes down, so what you keep stays exactly where you want it.

Cut material gets hauled off, or on the right property, ground down with our forestry mulching equipment instead of piled and burned. Either way the goal is the same: a clean, walkable property line and land that doesn't fight you every time you try to use it.

Brush Clearing or Forestry Mulching?

Hand-cut brush clearing and forestry mulching solve different versions of the same problem. Clearing works well on fence lines, property edges, and smaller patches where you want material cut and hauled clear. When the growth covers real acreage, our forestry mulching equipment can grind brush and small trees in place in a single pass, which is often the faster call on bigger jobs. We walk your property, tell you plainly which approach fits which part, and one estimate covers whichever tool the job needs. On land headed for a build, brush clearing is frequently the first step before lot preparation or grading.

If a stand of larger trees is mixed in with the undergrowth, that portion is a tree removal job, and we'll say so up front rather than let it slow down the brush work.

Brush Clearing FAQs

How much does brush clearing cost in New Orleans? +
It depends on how dense the growth is, how much ground you're clearing, and whether the material gets hauled off or mulched in place. Every property is different, which is why we walk the land before quoting. The estimate is free, fast, and accurate, so you get a real number before anything starts.
Will brush clearing stop regrowth for good? +
No cut is permanent in this climate, and brush comes back. Clearing knocks it back hard enough that regrowth is manageable instead of overwhelming, and cutting it low and clean the first time slows how fast it returns. Some property owners follow up with forestry mulching to keep it down longer.
Can you clear a fence line or property edge specifically? +
Yes. Fence lines and property corners are some of the most common brush clearing calls we get. We walk the line with you first so what comes down and what stays is clear before the crew starts.
Where do you offer brush clearing? +
New Orleans, Metairie, Baton Rouge, the Northshore, and surrounding southeast Louisiana communities.
