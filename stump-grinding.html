Why Not Just Leave It?

It keeps rotting

A buried root mass decays for years underground, whether you can see it or not.

The ground settles

It comes back

Sprouts push up through lawns for seasons off roots that never fully died.

Sod, pavers, a driveway, or a slab all need solid ground, not a stump underneath.

The method

How Does Stump Grinding Work?

A stump grinder is a wheel of carbide teeth that chews through wood a pass at a time, working the stump down from the top and out toward the edges. We take it below grade, not just flush with the surface, because flush is where the next problem starts. Surface roots close to the top get ground back too, so a mower or a footing doesn't hit them later.

What's left in the hole is a mix of wood chips and loose soil. On most jobs we backfill and rough-grade it level so the spot is ready for sod, gravel, or whatever comes next, instead of leaving a crater for you to deal with.

One Stump, or a Whole Lot of Them?

A single backyard stump is a quick visit. A lot full of them after a clearing job is a different scope, and often it makes sense to run stump grinding right behind tree removal in the same visit instead of scheduling it twice. If the ground is headed for a build, grinding stumps below grade is also a step ahead of lot preparation, so the grading crew isn't working around obstacles that should already be gone. One crew, one walk-through, one estimate that covers whatever combination your property needs.

Stump Grinding FAQs

How much does stump grinding cost in New Orleans? +
It comes down to how many stumps, how wide they are, and how easy the equipment can reach them. A single backyard stump and a lot full of them after a clearing job are two different quotes. The estimate is free, fast, and accurate, so you know the number before we start.
Why not just leave the stump in the ground? +
A stump left in place keeps rotting under whatever you put over it. Roots decay and leave voids, the ground above settles unevenly, and new sprouts can push up through a lawn for years. Grinding it below grade closes that out.
How deep do you grind a stump? +
Below the grade line, deep enough that sod, soil, gravel base, or a slab can go on top without sitting on rotting wood. How deep depends on what's going over it, and we talk through that on the estimate.
Can stump grinding happen right after tree removal? +
Yes, and most jobs run that way. One crew takes the tree down and grinds the stump in the same visit, so you are not left staring at a stump for weeks waiting on a second appointment.
Where do you offer stump grinding? +
New Orleans, Metairie, Baton Rouge, the Northshore, and surrounding southeast Louisiana communities.
