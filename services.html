What we cover

One Crew, Every Stage of the Job

A piece of Louisiana land rarely needs just one thing done to it. It needs the brush knocked back so you can see what's actually there, the dead or leaning trees taken down before the next storm tests them, the stumps ground out so a mower or a footer doesn't hit one, and the grade leveled so water runs off instead of pooling against a foundation. Some lots need an old shed or slab broken up and hauled away first. Some sit close enough to a wetland or a drainage line that the work has to be planned around it, not just done to it. All of that is land clearing, even though it looks like nine different jobs on paper.

We run all nine as one operation: tree removal, lot preparation, brush clearing, forestry mulching, stump grinding, grading and site leveling, demolition services, environmental services, and debris hauling. You don't call one company for the trees and another for the stumps. We walk the property, scope what it needs, quote the whole thing straight, and one crew handles it start to finish across New Orleans, Metairie, Baton Rouge, and the Northshore.
