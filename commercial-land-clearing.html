How it runs

How Does a Commercial Job Run?

Commercial clearing starts with a site walk against the plans, not a guess from a satellite photo. We look at what's standing, what needs to come down, where the grade has to move, and what an existing structure on the parcel means for the demolition side of the scope. From there the job covers what the site actually needs: tree removal and brush clearing to open the pad, stump grinding so grading equipment isn't fighting buried wood, grading and site leveling to the elevations on the plan, and debris hauling to close it out.

One crew running that whole list matters more on a commercial site than almost anywhere else. A GC juggling a foundation sub, a utility sub, and a paving sub doesn't need a fourth schedule for land clearing slipping on its own timeline. We work the dates you give us and hand back a graded, cleared parcel ready for the next trade.

Retail Pads, Multi-Family, and Industrial Sites

Whether it's a retail pad site, a multi-family parcel, or an industrial lot, the scope usually touches the same services: lot preparation to bring the site to a buildable state, and grading and site leveling to the elevations your engineer set, closed out with debris hauling so the site stays clear as work moves forward. If the parcel has an existing building on it, that scope folds in demolition services before clearing starts.

A site near wetlands or drainage brings a different set of questions into the plan up front, and that's where our environmental services approach comes in, worked out before a machine ever moves.
