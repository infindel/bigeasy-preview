Land Clearing in New Orleans, Done by One Crew

Clearing land in southeast Louisiana is its own kind of job. City lots in New Orleans and Metairie come with tight access, close neighbors, and utility lines exactly where you wish they weren't. Acreage on the Northshore and around Baton Rouge brings dense pine, wet ground, and undergrowth that swallows a property line. We work both every week, with the equipment matched to each: compact machines for tight side yards, forestry mulching for larger tracts, and full tree removal where a dead oak or storm-leaner has to come down first.

The scope runs from a single tree to a fully build-ready site. Most projects combine a few services under one estimate: clearing the growth, grinding the stumps, grading and leveling the pad, taking down an old shed, and hauling every piece of debris off the property. You deal with one crew and one number, not three subcontractors pointing at each other.

Storm season is part of the calendar here, and a lot of our calls start with a tree the last storm loosened. If that's you, don't wait for the next one to finish the job. Estimates are free and fast: we walk the property with you, quote it straight, and the number you hear is the number you pay. See what Louisiana homeowners and builders say about how that works out, or read our straight answers on cost, permits, and timing before you call.
