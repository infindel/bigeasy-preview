When you need it

When Do You Need Debris Hauling?

After a tree comes down

Trunks, limbs, and brush from a removal leave in the same trip, not stacked on your property for later.

After mulching or clearing

Material too big for the mulcher, or cleared brush that isn't staying as ground cover, hauled off the parcel.

After a demolition

Old shed, deck, or structure debris loaded out so the site is actually clear, not just knocked down.

Standalone cleanup

A property already has a pile from storm damage or older work, and you just need it gone.

The method

How Does Debris Hauling Work?

We load debris directly onto trucks and haul it off, instead of leaving material staged on your property for a second visit. Brush and limbs from a mulching job, whole trunks from a tree removal, lumber and rubble from a demolition: it goes out with the crew, the same day the work happens.

On combination jobs, hauling isn't a separate call to make later. When we clear a lot, take down a tree, or level a demo site, the material coming off the property is part of that same estimate. You end up with a clean parcel, not a clean parcel with a debris pile sitting at the edge of it.

Debris Hauling On Its Own, or Built Into the Job?

Some properties just need debris hauling by itself: storm debris, a stack of trunks somebody dropped and left, old fence material, or a brush pile that's outstayed its welcome. Others need it stitched into a bigger scope, like a tree removal that leaves whole trunks on the ground, or a demolition that leaves a structure in pieces where it stood. Either way, one estimate covers what has to leave the property, not just the work that knocked it down or cut it up. We walk the site with you, tell you plainly what's coming off and how, and quote the whole thing at once.

Straight answers

Debris Hauling FAQs

How much does debris hauling cost in New Orleans? +
It depends on the volume and type of material and how far it has to travel to disposal. Because hauling is often bundled into a tree removal, mulching, or demolition job, the estimate covers the whole scope. It's free, fast, and accurate: you get a real number before anything gets loaded.
Can you haul debris from a job someone else already did? +
Yes. Standalone hauling is common: storm debris, trunks another crew dropped and left, old fence material, or a brush pile that's been sitting since last season. You don't need to have hired us for the original work.
Does debris hauling include stump grinding? +
They're separate services that we're glad to combine into one estimate. Stump grinding turns the stump into grindings that stay on site; debris hauling is for material that needs to leave the property, like trunks, brush, and demo rubble.
Where do you offer debris hauling? +
New Orleans, Metairie, Baton Rouge, the Northshore, and surrounding southeast Louisiana communities.
